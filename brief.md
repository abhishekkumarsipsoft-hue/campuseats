# CampusEats — Project Brief

## What
CampusEats is a food ordering platform built for college campuses. It allows students 
to browse menus from on-campus eateries (canteens, cafes, food stalls), place orders 
online, make payments, and track their order status until delivery or pickup — 
removing the need to physically wait in long queues.

## Who
- **Students** — browse menus, place orders, make payments, track delivery
- **Vendors/Eateries** — manage their menu, receive and update orders
- **Delivery staff** — pick up orders and update delivery status
- **Admin** — oversees the platform, manages vendors and users

## Nouns (entities / resources)
- Student
- Vendor
- MenuItem
- Order
- Payment
- Delivery

## Verbs (actions / tasks)
- browseMenu — view available food items from a vendor
- placeOrder — create a new order with selected items
- makePayment — pay for an order
- updateOrderStatus — vendor marks order as preparing/ready
- trackDelivery — student checks live delivery status
- cancelOrder — student cancels an order before it's prepared
